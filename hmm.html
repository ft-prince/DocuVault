📞 Frequently Asked Contact Questions

How quickly will I get a response?

We respond to all inquiries within 4 business hours on weekdays. Urgent requests related to compliance deadlines are prioritized and typically answered within 1 hour.

Is the assessment really free?

Yes, 100% free with no obligation. We visit your facility, evaluate opportunities, and provide a detailed assessment report. You only pay if you decide to proceed with implementation.

Do you serve facilities outside Gurgaon/Delhi NCR?

Absolutely! We serve manufacturing facilities across India. Our team travels to client sites nationwide for installation, audits, and support. We have active projects in Maharashtra, Gujarat, Tamil Nadu, Karnataka, and more.

Can I speak to someone immediately?

Yes, call us at +91 98102 17013 during business hours (Mon-Sat, 10 AM - 6 PM). For after-hours inquiries, WhatsApp is the fastest option, and we'll respond first thing next business day.

What information should I prepare before contacting?

Helpful to have: monthly electricity bills, approximate CO₂ emissions (if known), facility size/production capacity, and any specific compliance requirements. But don't worry - we can work with whatever information you have available.
