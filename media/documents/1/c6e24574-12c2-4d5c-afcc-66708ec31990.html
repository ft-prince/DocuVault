Queue Analysis & Queue Length Detection

Monitor queue lengths and wait times at checkout counters, service desks, and entry points to optimize customer experience and resource allocation.

No PPE Kit Detection

Automatically detect workers without proper Personal Protective Equipment like helmets, vests, masks, and gloves to ensure workplace safety compliance.

Perimeter / Intrusion Detection

Virtual fence monitoring with instant alerts for unauthorized entry into restricted areas, boundary breaches, and perimeter violations.

Boundary Loitering Detection

Critical security feature to detect weapons like guns, knives, or rifles in real-time and trigger immediate emergency alerts.
